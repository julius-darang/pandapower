**What v3 delivers:** A 3-panel analytical dashboard —
- **Network Topology** (left): geodata diagram with loading-aware line coloring (green ≤ 100%, red > 100%), bus voltage annotations, and P/Q labels.
- **Voltage Profile** (centre): line plot with a ±5% normal operating band and exact per-unit values at each bus.
- **Line Loading** (right): bar chart with a 100% thermal limit line and automatic bar labels.

Loading-aware logic means the dashboard tells you where the problem is before you even read a number.
